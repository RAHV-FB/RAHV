How buying in Barcelona works

What's different from a U.S. purchase?

The representation model, the paperwork and the timeline. There isn't the buyer-broker setup you know from the States, and some roles, like the notary, have no exact U.S. equivalent.

What should happen before serious viewings?

A clear picture of budget, the kind of property you want, and two or three areas to focus on. Much of this can be done before you fly over.

How does an offer progress?

Interest usually moves to a reservation, then a deposit agreement (the arras), then the final deed at the notary. The local team coordinates each step.

What can be done remotely?

Video viewings, document review and much of the coordination can happen from abroad.

What about older buildings and community costs?

With older buildings, look at the community as well as the apartment: fees, communal areas and any planned works.

Who handles legal and tax?

Qualified professionals. I can introduce you to them; I don't give legal, tax, mortgage or financial advice.

This page is general information, not legal, tax or mortgage advice. Figures, deadlines and costs should be confirmed with the appropriate professionals.
