Barcelona and nearby areas

Barcelona, the metropolitan area, Sitges, Castelldefels and the Costa Brava.

Barcelona City

Typical housing
Mostly apartments, from Eixample flats to much older buildings.
Feel
Dense, walkable, fully urban.
Tends to suit
Buyers who want to be in the city.
Worth checking first
The building: age, communal areas, fees and any works.

Metropolitan Barcelona

Typical housing
Houses and gardens become realistic, in areas such as Sant Just Desvern.
Feel
Quieter and residential, still connected.
Tends to suit
Families, and anyone wanting outdoor space.
Worth checking first
The connection to the city for your routine. It varies by municipality.

Sitges & Castelldefels

Typical housing
Coastal apartments and homes, seafront to hillside.
Feel
Relaxed, near the sea, within reach of Barcelona.
Tends to suit
Second homes, and buyers who want the coast close.
Worth checking first
How the area feels off-season.

Costa Brava

Typical housing
Coastal houses among coves and pine.
Feel
Scenic and seasonal.
Tends to suit
Second homes and getaways.
Worth checking first
Access and services outside the summer months.
