Privacy policy & legal notice

Who we are

Your Home Barcelona is run by Steve Howard, based in Barcelona, Spain. For anything relating to this site or your data, write to hello@yourhomebarcelona.com.

What we collect, and why

When you send an enquiry we collect your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, budget range and anything else you choose to share. We use it to respond to you and help with your property search.

We keep your details only as long as your enquiry is active or as the law requires, and we never sell them.

Who we may share it with

To move your search forward, relevant details may be shared with the licensed local real-estate professionals we work with, and with the service providers that run this site.

The booking calendar

The home, contact and scheduling pages include a booking calendar provided by Cal.com, which we use to let you book a call. When one of those pages loads, your browser connects to Cal.com to display the calendar; that connection is handled under Cal.com's own privacy policy.

If you book a time, the details you enter, such as your name and email, are processed by Cal.com and shared with us so we can hold the call. We use the calendar only for scheduling, not for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@yourhomebarcelona.com. Consent is never pre-selected on our forms, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and loads no advertising or analytics trackers. The Cal.com booking calendar described above may set its own functional cookies when you use it.

A note on advice

Steve Howard is a former licensed real-estate agent in California and Florida and is not a licensed estate agent in Catalonia. Property searches, viewings and transactions are carried out by licensed local professionals. Nothing on this site is legal, tax, mortgage or financial advice.
