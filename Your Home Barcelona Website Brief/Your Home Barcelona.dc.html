The difference

Buying in Barcelona works differently from the U.S.

In the U.S. you know the process — how offers work, who represents whom, what closing looks like. In Spain the terminology, the representation and the timeline are different.

I've lived in Barcelona for about twenty years and worked in real estate in the U.S. I can explain the differences, then bring in the licensed local professionals who handle the search and the transaction.

About

Steve Howard

I'm Steve Howard, and I've lived in Barcelona since 2006. Before moving to Spain, I worked in real estate in California and held real-estate licences in California and Florida. Those licences are no longer active, and I'm not a licensed estate agent in Catalonia.

I came for a Spanish woman and the city, and stayed. I built my family and my life here.

I help buyers understand Barcelona — the neighbourhoods, the property, and how buying differs from the U.S. When it's time for the regulated work — the search, viewings and the transaction — I work with a licensed local real-estate network. I don't provide legal, tax, mortgage or financial advice.
